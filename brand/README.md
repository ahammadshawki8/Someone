# Someone: brand

**Name:** Someone. **Tagline:** Be the someone who moves first.

**Mark:** a thin coral ring around a solid coral dot. It is the product's one moment (the pace ring around the hand target on the chest), a pulse, and one person singled out from a crowd. Files: `mark.svg`, `logo-dark.svg`, `logo-light.svg`.

**Wordmark:** "someone", lowercase, Inter SemiBold, tight tracking (-2 at 72 px).

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Pulse coral | `#FF5A4E` | the mark, the pace ring and target, the heartbeat. Never decoration. |
| On-pace mint | `#5CF2B5` | success only: on pace, "You did not freeze.", the view share frame |
| Caution amber | `#FFC24D` | caution only: drifting rate, the AED chevron |
| Warm white | `#F7F5F2` | all text |
| Ink | `#0B0B0C` | video and page backgrounds (never inside the Lens: black is transparent on Specs) |
| Slate | `#8A8F98` | secondary text in the video and docs |

**Type:** Inter (SemiBold for headlines, Medium for body), sentence case, short verbs.

**Motion:** slow eases (300 to 500 ms), fades and gentle rises. The pulse at 110 per minute is the only rhythm.
