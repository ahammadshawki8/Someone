# Someone: film script (under 3:00)

Documentary structure: hook, problem, turn, walkthrough, why it wins, close. Narration is generated with Kokoro-82M (`tools/kokoro/gen_narration.mjs`, voice `af_bella`) as a stand-in; the owner can re-record any line with the same timing.

Footage: the Lens Studio preview recorded live (LEAF `demo-run-scenario` in the Sunlit Outdoor plaza), our own Remotion graphics, and licensed stock (Pexels and Mixkit, credited at the end). Score: "Silent Descent" (Mixkit, free license). Sound design (heartbeat, monitor, flatline) synthesized locally.
