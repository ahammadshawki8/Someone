# Someone: demo video script (under 3:00)

Narration is generated with Kokoro-82M (`tools/kokoro/gen_narration.mjs`) as a stand-in; the owner can re-record any line with the same timing. All footage is the Lens Studio preview recorded on this machine, plus our own Remotion cards. No stock, no music, no logos.

| # | Section | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | Cold open | Black, one line at a time: "Someone collapses." / "A crowd forms." / "Everyone waits." | When someone collapses, a crowd forms. And everyone waits for someone else to act. |
| N2 | The problem | Three stat cards: 4 in 10 get bystander CPR / 37% of trained bystanders who did not help said they panicked / about 10% less survival every minute | In the US, only about four in ten cardiac arrest patients get CPR from a bystander. Of trained bystanders who didn't help, thirty seven percent said they simply panicked. And every minute without CPR, the chance of survival drops by about ten percent. |
| N3 | The product | Title: Someone. Three pillars: hands free on the chest, guidance drawn on the body, 911 sees your view | Someone is a Lens for Snap Specs that turns a frozen bystander into the someone. One calm step at a time, both hands free, with nine one one seeing what you see. |
| N4 | Check and call | Preview: Someone collapsed, Tap their shoulder, Call 911, badge | It only ever shows one instruction. Check them. Call nine one one. The dispatcher connects, sees your view, and talks you through it. |
| N5 | The moment | Preview: target and ring on the chest, mint ring, "Good." | A target sits on the center of the chest, at real size. Your hands push, and on Specs, hand tracking counts every stroke. The ring pulses at a hundred and ten a minute, the beat comes from the chest, and it turns mint when you're on pace. |
| N6 | The crowd | Preview: AED chevron, "Point at one person." | At ninety seconds, a chime and an arrow point to the A E D. And Someone never asks the crowd. It tells you to point at one person. That's how you break the bystander effect. |
| N7 | AED | Preview: swap, pads, Stand clear, resume | It prompts a swap at two minutes, shows where the pads go, and keeps you clear for the shock. |
| N8 | Replay | Preview: replay | When help arrives, a quiet replay. And one line. You did not freeze. |
| N9 | How it was built | Cards: design brief, the agent in Lens Studio through the MCP, 8 LEAF scenarios passing | Someone was planned with the plan lens skill, built end to end by a coding agent through CLAD and the Lens Studio MCP, and checked by eight LEAF tests that run in the preview. |
| N10 | Close | Pulsing ring on black. "In a crowd, everyone waits. You won't." / "Someone. Be the someone." | In a crowd, everyone waits. You won't. |

Stand-ins named on screen: the manikin, the pinch that stands in for a push in the preview, the scripted dispatcher, AED and shock.
