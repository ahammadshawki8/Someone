Ours: the Lens Studio preview recordings (`footage/`), narration (Kokoro-82M, Apache 2.0, `tools/kokoro/gen_narration.mjs`), sound design (`tools/gen_film_sfx.js`), the brand mark and all graphics.

Proxies were made with ffmpeg: 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264, first 8 to 16 s of each clip.
